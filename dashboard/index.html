    Method & caveats

    Read these before quoting a number.

    Data coverage

    Consumption volumes run January 2024 to February 2026 (26 months). Anything after that is forecast. Price series from 2026 are press-sourced pump-price announcements, not an official time series.

    A labelling error in the source

    The LeadAfrik consumption file labels petrol as "LPG" and LPG as "Other". Columns were relabelled after reconciliation against annual totals. Jet fuel and fuel oil were identified by magnitude only, so they carry an asterisk throughout.

    What is an assumption, not data

    South Lokichar economics (prices, costs, PSC terms), the 30-day cover standard, risk weights and thresholds, and scenario elasticities are the author's assumptions. They are tagged where shown.

    Forecast method

    Eight candidate models per product were compared on 33 rolling-origin forecasts using sMAPE and MASE. The best model was used; the intervals come from its rolling errors. A 26-month history is short, so treat the intervals as indicative.

    Colour and access

    Series colours were checked for colour-vision separation in both themes. Every chart has hover values, and the main tables repeat the data.
